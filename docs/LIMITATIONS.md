# FaultLens: Limitations

*Detailed limitations will be documented during validation in Session 5.*
- Uploaded CSVs have no alarm codes, so the dashboard fills a placeholder alarm for each logged reason, and the alarm agreement score is not informative for uploads.
- Synthetic-only evaluation: Real industrial plants contain unmodeled mechanical interactions.
- Scope boundary: 4 machines, 6 causes, 4 controlled defect types.
- Abstention trade-off: High defect severity prompts abstention; where evidence is corrupted beyond recovery, human inspection is mandated.
